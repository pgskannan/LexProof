LexProof: Verifiable Legal Intelligence
AI finds the risk. LexProof proves what happened.

AI contract review is fast, but it leaves no proof. LexProof turns every review into verifiable evidence. AI flags risks and proposes redlines, people approve them, and the result is sealed into a Legal Passport whose SHA-256 fingerprint is anchored on Ethereum. Anyone can verify it in their own browser, without trusting LexProof.
